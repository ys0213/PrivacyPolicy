Privacy Policy

Game: Animal School Mystery - The Missing Carrot
Developer: YuSun Lim
Privacy contact: minimaldev.contact@gmail.com
Last updated: October 22, 2026

This policy explains how information is handled when you use Animal School Mystery - The Missing Carrot on Android, including optional rewarded advertising and optional in-app purchases.

1. Game data stored on your device

The game does not require an account. Its story progress, clue state, Detective Keys (including the locally tracked free and purchased balances), Extras progress, language choice, audio settings, and records of fulfilled in-app purchases are saved locally by the app. The developer does not operate a game account server or a remote gameplay database for this title.

For duplicate-reward prevention, the app stores a one-way SHA-256 hash of each fulfilled Google Play purchase token in local game data. It does not store the raw purchase token in the local reward history. Local data may be removed by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app, subject to any device-level backup and restore settings.

2. Advertising and data processed by third parties

The game uses Unity LevelPlay for optional rewarded advertisements. The rewarded-ad unit's reviewed mediation configuration connects ironSource Ads as its advertising network. The Android package also includes the Unity Ads SDK and mediation adapter; Unity Ads was not connected as a serving network for this rewarded-ad unit in the configuration reviewed for this policy. The presence of an SDK in the package does not by itself mean that network serves an ad.

When optional advertising is enabled, the advertising technology may process or share information including approximate location, interactions with advertisements, application diagnostics, and device or other identifiers. According to ironSource Ads' published Google Data Safety Questionnaire, these categories support advertising or marketing, analytics, and fraud prevention, security, or compliance. Processing can vary with the SDK, device settings, and applicable privacy rules. The game does not send its local story progress or clues to a developer-operated account service.

Advertising and SDK privacy details: Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy, ironSource Ads Google Data Safety Questionnaire, and Unity Ads Google Play Data Safety disclosure.

3. Optional in-app purchases

The game offers a voluntary, repeatable Support the Developer purchase through Google Play Billing. Each completed purchase grants five Detective Keys as a thank-you. Purchasing is not required to play the main story. The price and currency shown at checkout are provided by Google Play for the purchasing account's region.

This is a consumable in-app product, not a subscription or a permanent entitlement. Detective Keys and the local purchase-fulfillment history remain on this device. Once a purchase has been fulfilled and consumed in Google Play, its previously granted Keys cannot be reliably restored after deleting app data, uninstalling without a restorable backup, or moving to another device. Game-progress reset inside the app is different from deleting Android app data.

Google Play handles the platform purchase flow, payments, and associated transaction processing. The developer does not collect payment-card numbers through this game or run a separate payment server. See Google Privacy Policy for Google's data practices.

The game integrates Unity In-App Purchasing 5.4.4. Under Unity's published disclosures for IAP 5.4 and later, the SDK collects or processes data types such as approximate location/country, email address, personal or device identifiers, purchase history, crash logs, app diagnostics, and performance information for app functionality and analytics, subject to Unity's documented data handling. These SDK activities are separate from the game's local-only story saves and do not create a player account. Refer to Unity IAP privacy overview and Unity IAP Google Play data safety disclosure.

4. How data may be used or shared

Advertising-related information may be transferred to Unity/ironSource and relevant advertising service providers to deliver and measure ads, analyze performance, and help prevent fraud or maintain security. Payment-related information is processed through Google Play and the Unity IAP SDK according to their published practices, including purchase delivery, app functionality, and applicable analytics. The information processed and parties involved depend on the SDK components and services actually used. Their respective privacy notices describe further processing, disclosure, transfer, and retention practices. The developer does not operate a separate server that receives individual players' local save data.

5. Choices and privacy rights

Rewarded advertisements are optional. You can allow or decline them and change your preference at any time through Settings > Ads & Privacy without restarting the game. Your choice is stored on your device. Declining optional ads does not prevent you from playing the main story or using rewards you already earned. An ad-based reward is granted only after its qualifying advertisement is successfully completed.

The game uses your saved advertising preference to control subsequent advertising initialization and requests, and sends privacy choice signals to the supported advertising SDK. A previously initialized SDK may remain active during the current session, and changing your choice does not automatically erase data a provider already processed. The ad preference does not itself govern Google Play purchases or Unity IAP data processing. Unity IAP does not provide its own consent service; any consent or opt-out obligations for this SDK require separate consideration under applicable law.

You can contact minimaldev.contact@gmail.com with privacy questions or requests. Depending on local law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or object to certain processing. Advertising, payment, and SDK providers may have their own request and privacy-choice procedures.

6. Data storage, retention and deletion

Game progress, Keys, and settings remain in the app's local storage until you clear app data, uninstall the app, or they are otherwise removed under your device's backup and restore settings. We do not maintain a developer-operated cloud copy of your mystery progress or of previously consumed Detective Keys. Advertising, payment, and SDK providers separately determine retention and deletion of data they process under their own policies. For assistance or to make a privacy request, email minimaldev.contact@gmail.com. We can explain the game's local data practices and direct provider-specific requests to the relevant published process when appropriate.

7. Security and international processing

The game does not upload local story saves to a developer-operated server. The advertising and Unity IAP providers' published SDK disclosures state that the data collected by their respective SDKs is encrypted in transit. Advertising and commerce services may process data outside your country of residence, as described in their policies. These provider statements do not by themselves certify every aspect of the app's security.

8. Audience

This game's declared target audience is people aged 13 and older. The game does not provide user accounts or ask for a player's date of birth. Additional privacy protections may apply to minors depending on local law.

9. Updates and contact

We may update this policy when game features, advertising or payment services, or applicable requirements change. The updated date will be shown on this page. Contact minimaldev.contact@gmail.com with questions.
