Privacy Policy

Game: Animal School Mystery - The Missing Carrot
Developer: YuSun Lim
Privacy contact: minimaldev.contact@gmail.com
Draft reviewed: October 14, 2026

This policy explains how information is handled when you use Animal School Mystery - The Missing Carrot on Android, including optional rewarded advertising.

1. Game data stored on your device

The game does not require an account. Its story progress, clue state, Detective Keys, Extras progress, language choice, and audio settings are saved locally by the app. The developer does not operate a game account server or a remote gameplay database for this title.

Local data may be removed by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app, subject to any device-level backup and restore settings.

2. Advertising and data processed by third parties

The game includes Unity LevelPlay with ironSource Ads for optional rewarded advertising. When this advertising technology is used, its SDK may collect and share data such as approximate location, interactions with advertisements, app diagnostics, and device or other identifiers. According to the provider's published Data safety questionnaire, these data types can support ad delivery, analytics, and fraud prevention, security and compliance.

The specific data involved can vary with SDK operation, device settings, jurisdiction, and permissions. The developer does not receive a list of individual players or their locally saved mystery progress through an account system.

Advertising provider details: Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy and ironSource Ads Google Data Safety Questionnaire.

3. How data may be used or shared

Advertising-related data may be processed by Unity/ironSource and their service providers for advertising services, measuring ad interactions, diagnostics, and security or fraud prevention. The SDK provider describes its own processing, transfers and retention practices in the linked privacy policy.

4. Choices and privacy rights

Watching rewarded ads is optional. If you do not watch an ad, you will not receive the related advertising reward. You can contact minimaldev.contact@gmail.com with privacy questions or requests. Depending on your region, rights may include accessing, deleting or objecting to certain personal data processing. For data controlled by an advertising provider, its own privacy procedures may also apply.

Release review pending: The game's region-specific advertising consent and opt-out implementation must be verified before the final wording of this section is approved.

5. Data storage, retention and deletion

Game progress data remains in local app storage subject to the user's device settings and app-data deletion. Third-party advertising information is handled according to the provider's applicable policies and retention practices. For help with privacy-related requests, use the contact address above.

6. Security and international processing

The game does not transmit its local story save to a developer-operated server. The advertising SDK provider states that data collected by its SDK is encrypted in transit; its services may involve processing in countries outside the user's country of residence. See the provider's linked privacy policy for details.

7. Audience

This game's declared target audience is people aged 13 and older. The game does not provide user accounts or ask for a player's date of birth. Additional privacy protections may apply to minors depending on local law.

8. Updates and contact

We may update this policy when game features, advertising services or applicable requirements change. The updated date will be shown on this page. Contact minimaldev.contact@gmail.com with questions.
