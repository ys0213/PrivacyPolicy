Privacy Policy

Game: Animal School Mystery - The Missing Carrot
Developer: YuSun Lim
Privacy contact: minimaldev.contact@gmail.com
Last updated: October 16, 2026

This policy explains how information is handled when you use Animal School Mystery - The Missing Carrot on Android, including optional rewarded advertising.

1. Game data stored on your device

The game does not require an account. Its story progress, clue state, Detective Keys, Extras progress, language choice, and audio settings are saved locally by the app. The developer does not operate a game account server or a remote gameplay database for this title.

Local data may be removed by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app, subject to any device-level backup and restore settings.

2. Advertising and data processed by third parties

The game uses Unity LevelPlay for optional rewarded advertisements. The rewarded-ad unit's reviewed mediation configuration connects ironSource Ads as its advertising network. The Android package also includes the Unity Ads SDK and mediation adapter; Unity Ads was not connected as a serving network for this rewarded-ad unit in the configuration reviewed for this policy. The presence of an SDK in the package does not by itself mean that network serves an ad.

When optional advertising is enabled, the advertising technology may process or share information including approximate location, interactions with advertisements, application diagnostics, and device or other identifiers. According to ironSource Ads' published Google Data Safety Questionnaire, these categories support advertising or marketing, analytics, and fraud prevention, security, or compliance. Processing can vary with the SDK, device settings, and applicable privacy rules. The game does not send its local story progress or clues to a developer-operated account service.

Advertising and SDK privacy details: Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy, ironSource Ads Google Data Safety Questionnaire, and Unity Ads Google Play Data Safety disclosure.

3. How data may be used or shared

Advertising-related information may be transferred to Unity/ironSource and relevant advertising service providers to deliver and measure ads, analyze performance, and help prevent fraud or maintain security. The information processed and parties involved depend on the advertising components actually used. Their respective privacy notices describe further processing, disclosure, transfer, and retention practices. The developer does not operate a separate server that receives individual players' local save data.

4. Choices and privacy rights

Rewarded advertisements are optional. You can allow or decline them and change your preference at any time through Settings > Ads & Privacy without restarting the game. Your choice is stored on your device. Declining optional ads does not prevent you from playing the main story or using rewards you already earned. An ad-based reward is granted only after its qualifying advertisement is successfully completed.

The game uses your saved advertising preference to control subsequent advertising initialization and requests, and sends privacy choice signals to the supported advertising SDK. A previously initialized SDK may remain active during the current session, and changing your choice does not automatically erase data a provider already processed. You can contact minimaldev.contact@gmail.com with privacy questions or requests. Depending on local law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or object to certain processing. Advertising providers may also have their own request and privacy-choice procedures.

5. Data storage, retention and deletion

Game progress and settings remain in the app's local storage until you clear app data, uninstall the app, or they are otherwise removed under your device's backup and restore settings. We do not maintain a developer-operated cloud copy of your mystery progress. Advertising providers separately determine retention and deletion of data they process under their own policies. For assistance or to make a privacy request, email minimaldev.contact@gmail.com. We can explain the game's local data practices and direct provider-specific requests to the relevant published process when appropriate.

6. Security and international processing

The game does not upload local story saves to a developer-operated server. The advertising providers' published SDK disclosures state that data collected by their respective SDKs is encrypted in transit. Advertising services may process data outside your country of residence, as described in their policies. These provider statements do not by themselves certify every aspect of the app's security.

7. Audience

This game's declared target audience is people aged 13 and older. The game does not provide user accounts or ask for a player's date of birth. Additional privacy protections may apply to minors depending on local law.

8. Updates and contact

We may update this policy when game features, advertising services or applicable requirements change. The updated date will be shown on this page. Contact minimaldev.contact@gmail.com with questions.

This policy covers Animal School Mystery - The Missing Carrot on Android. It is separate from the privacy notices of the advertising services linked above.
