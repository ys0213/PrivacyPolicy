Privacy Policy

Game: Animal School Mystery 3 — After-School Escape
Platform: Android
Developer: YuSun Lim
Privacy contact: minimaldev.contact@gmail.com
Last updated: November 19, 2026

This policy describes how information is handled in Animal School Mystery 3 — After-School Escape. It applies only to this game, not to the first or second Animal School Mystery games or other apps published by the developer.

1. Local game data

The game does not require an account, login, name, email address, or date of birth. It saves the following information locally on your device so that you can continue playing:

  • Story progress, current scene, dialogue position, visited locations, investigations, discovered clues and records, and read dialogue.
  • Detective Key balances, first-play and first-clear rewards, detailed-hint unlocks, and unlocks for the three Extra Stories.
  • Selected language (English, Korean, or Japanese), audio volume, dialogue playback preferences, and related settings.

These files are stored in the app's device storage. The developer does not operate a game-account server or a separate remote database for these game saves.

2. Optional rewarded advertising

Optional feature: This version of Animal School Mystery 3 integrates the Unity LevelPlay (Ads Mediation) SDK for optional rewarded advertisements. The app does not require you to watch ads to play the main story. Ad availability depends on network configuration, consent choices, your device, and whether a provider has an ad available.

When you allow rewarded ads, Unity/ironSource and participating ad networks may collect, use or share information such as device identifiers and advertising identifiers, approximate country or location, app and device information, ad interaction or other app-activity data, and diagnostics. Purposes may include delivering and measuring ads, analytics, fraud prevention, and security. The participating networks and the information they process depend on the ad network configuration and the ads actually delivered. Installing an adapter does not establish that a specific network served an ad.

Provider notices: Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy, ironSource Ads Google Data Safety Questionnaire, and Unity Ads Google Play Data Safety.

If a rewarded video is displayed and the advertising SDK confirms its earned reward, the game adds one Detective Key to your locally stored Key balance. Allowing ads, tapping the ad button, loading an ad, or closing a video without earning a reward does not award a Key. The game keeps local reward-request identifiers to prevent repeated grants for the same request.

3. Optional purchases

Developer-support payments and Google Play in-app purchases are not active in this version. Any menu entry referring to support is informational only. No purchase occurs through that entry. If payments are activated in a later release, this policy must be updated before that release.

4. Third-party platform services

Android, Google Play, the Unity game engine and the included LevelPlay advertising service may handle technical information as part of app installation, operation, and optional ad delivery, subject to their notices and the services enabled in the release. The developer does not upload locally saved story progress or clue records to a developer-operated server.

For details about the platform providers, see the Google Privacy Policy and Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy. Those provider policies describe their broader practices; not every feature described in them is necessarily enabled in this game.

5. How locally stored information is used

Local game data is used to resume the story, display the casebook and clues, remember unlocked content and Detective Keys, save ad preferences and reward records, and preserve settings. The game does not require you to submit these records to the developer.

6. Advertising choices and privacy requests

You can decide whether to enable optional rewarded ads in the game through Menu > Settings > Ad settings. This local choice is saved on your device and passed to the supported LevelPlay consent API. Declining or turning off rewarded ads does not stop the main story or remove previously earned Detective Keys or unlocked Extra Stories.

Turning off ads stops the game from requesting new rewarded ads through the integrated ad feature. It does not erase information already processed by a third-party provider, and changes may not affect an advertisement already in progress. The in-game preference is not a replacement for any additional legally required regional consent-management or provider-specific privacy controls.

If you have questions or wish to exercise applicable privacy rights, contact minimaldev.contact@gmail.com. Ad providers may offer separate controls and data-request processes in their own notices. Advertising preferences do not control Google Play's platform-level data handling.

7. Retention, reset, and deletion

Local game progress and settings generally remain on the device until removed by an available in-game operation, clearing the app's data, or uninstalling the app, subject to Android backup and restore behavior.

Resetting story progress inside the game is not the same as deleting all app data. In-game story reset preserves separately stored Detective Keys, Extra Story and hint unlocks, and settings. To remove locally stored game data more completely, use your Android device's app-storage controls. Data processed by advertising and platform providers is subject to their own retention and deletion processes.

8. Security and international processing

The game does not send its local save files to a developer-operated cloud server. Advertising and platform providers may process information in countries other than your own according to their respective notices and safeguards. This policy does not guarantee any particular security or backup outcome.

9. Children and families

The game does not request a player's age, name, or contact information. Parents and guardians who have privacy-related questions may use the contact address below. The intended age group and applicable advertising safeguards must be checked against the actual Google Play target-audience declarations and child-protection requirements before release. Parents or guardians may contact us about privacy concerns.

10. Changes and contact

This policy may change if the game's features, third-party services, or applicable legal requirements change. The date above will be updated when a revised policy is published.

For privacy inquiries, contact minimaldev.contact@gmail.com.

This policy applies to Animal School Mystery 3 — After-School Escape on Android only. It describes the proposed optional LevelPlay rewarded-ad release. Actual SDK adapters and consent management must be verified before publishing the app; purchases are not yet implemented.
