Privacy Policy

Game: Animal School Mystery 3 — After-School Escape
Platform: Android
Developer: YuSun Lim
Privacy contact: minimaldev.contact@gmail.com
Last updated: November 19, 2026

This policy describes how information is handled in Animal School Mystery 3 — After-School Escape. It applies only to this game, not to the first or second Animal School Mystery games or other apps published by the developer.

1. Local game data

The game does not require an account, login, name, email address, or date of birth. It saves the following information locally on your device so that you can continue playing:

  • Story progress, current scene, dialogue position, visited locations, investigations, discovered clues and records, and read dialogue.
  • Detective Key balances, first-play and first-clear rewards, detailed-hint unlocks, and unlocks for the three Extra Stories.
  • Selected language (English, Korean, or Japanese), audio volume, dialogue playback preferences, and related settings.

These files are stored in the app's device storage. The developer does not operate a game-account server or a separate remote database for these game saves.

2. Current status of advertising and purchases

At the time of this policy: Rewarded advertising and developer-support purchases are not active in this game's current implementation. The game may show menu entries or buttons for these planned features, but those entries do not currently provide an advertising-consent manager or process a payment.

The game's local save format reserves fields for rewarded-ad request identifiers and records of completed purchases, including SHA-256 hashes derived from purchase tokens. Those fields support prevention of duplicate rewards if the relevant features are implemented later. Their existence does not mean that an advertisement was served or a purchase was made.

If a future release enables rewarded advertising, Google Play purchases, or an associated consent-management service, this policy will be updated to describe the services actually included and the information they process before those features are released.

3. Third-party platform services

Android, Google Play, and the Unity game engine may handle technical information as part of app installation, operation, and platform services, subject to the services and settings in the particular release. The developer does not upload locally saved story progress or clue records to a developer-operated server.

For details about the platform providers, see the Google Privacy Policy and Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy. These links do not mean every product or optional advertising feature described in those policies is enabled in this game.

4. How locally stored information is used

Local game data is used to resume the story, display the casebook and clues, remember unlocked content and Detective Keys, and preserve settings. The game does not require you to submit these records to the developer.

5. Advertising and purchase choices

There is no active rewarded-ad consent interface or in-game purchase checkout in the version described here. You can play the main story without watching advertisements or buying Detective Keys. You may review applicable device and Google Play privacy controls through your device settings.

If advertising or purchases are added in a later version, relevant consent or privacy-choice controls, third-party notices, purchase terms, and this policy will need to be updated. A future rewarded advertisement should only grant a Detective Key after its reward is verified; an uncompleted ad or consent selection alone is not a reward.

6. Retention, reset, and deletion

Local game progress and settings generally remain on the device until removed by an available in-game operation, clearing the app's data, or uninstalling the app, subject to Android backup and restore behavior.

Resetting story progress inside the game is not the same as deleting all app data. In-game story reset preserves separately stored Detective Keys, Extra Story and hint unlocks, and settings. To remove locally stored game data more completely, use your Android device's app-storage controls. Data already processed by platform providers, if any, is subject to their own retention and deletion processes.

7. Security and international processing

The game does not send its local save files to a developer-operated cloud server. Platform providers may process information in countries other than your own according to their respective notices and safeguards. This policy does not guarantee any particular security or backup outcome.

8. Children and families

The game does not request a player's age, name, or contact information. Parents and guardians who have privacy-related questions may use the contact address below. Any advertising or purchase functionality introduced for a future release must be assessed against that release's Google Play target-audience declarations and applicable children's privacy requirements.

9. Changes and contact

This policy may change if the game's features, third-party services, or applicable legal requirements change. The date above will be updated when a revised policy is published.

For privacy inquiries, contact minimaldev.contact@gmail.com.

This policy applies to Animal School Mystery 3 — After-School Escape on Android only. It reflects the game's currently implemented features, and must be reviewed if advertising, purchases, or additional network services are enabled.
