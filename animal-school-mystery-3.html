Privacy Policy

Game: Animal School Mystery 3 — After-School Escape
Platform: Android
Developer: YuSun Lim
Privacy contact: minimaldev.contact@gmail.com
Last updated: November 20, 2026

This policy describes how information is handled in Animal School Mystery 3 — After-School Escape. It applies only to this game, not to the first or second Animal School Mystery games or other apps published by the developer.

1. Local game data

The game does not require an account, login, name, email address, or date of birth. It saves the following information locally on your device so that you can continue playing:

  • Story progress, current scene, dialogue position, visited locations, investigations, discovered clues and records, and read dialogue.
  • Detective Key balances, first-play and first-clear rewards, detailed-hint unlocks, and unlocks for the three Extra Stories.
  • Selected language (English, Korean, or Japanese), audio volume, dialogue playback preferences, and related settings.

These files are stored in the app's device storage. The developer does not operate a game-account server or a separate remote database for these game saves.

2. Optional rewarded advertising

Optional feature: This version of Animal School Mystery 3 integrates the Unity LevelPlay (Ads Mediation) SDK for optional rewarded advertisements. The app does not require you to watch ads to play the main story. Ad availability depends on network configuration, consent choices, your device, and whether a provider has an ad available.

At the date of this policy, the game's rewarded-ad mediation configuration includes ironSource Ads and Unity Ads. When you enable rewarded ads, these providers and their partners may collect, use, or share information such as device and advertising identifiers, approximate location (such as country), app and device information, ad interactions and other app activity, and diagnostics. Purposes may include ad delivery and measurement, analytics, fraud prevention, and security. The information processed depends on the ads actually served and the providers involved. Having an SDK or adapter installed does not mean that a particular provider served you an ad.

Provider notices: Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy, ironSource Ads Google Data Safety Questionnaire, and Unity Ads Google Play Data Safety.

If a rewarded video is displayed and the advertising SDK confirms its earned reward, the game adds one Detective Key to your locally stored Key balance. Allowing ads, tapping the ad button, loading an ad, or closing a video without earning a reward does not award a Key. The game keeps local reward-request identifiers to prevent repeated grants for the same request.

3. Optional in-app purchases

The game offers an optional, repeatable Support the Developer purchase through Google Play. Each successfully verified and fulfilled purchase awards five Detective Keys. The product is a consumable one-time purchase, not a subscription. The price and currency displayed before purchase are provided by Google Play. The main story does not require a purchase.

Google Play processes the payment and transaction data. The app uses Unity In-App Purchasing (IAP) 5.4.4 for the purchase flow and may process purchase receipts, product and transaction identifiers, and necessary device and app information to deliver purchases, verify receipts, prevent duplicate rewards and support purchase reporting. See Unity IAP privacy overview, Unity IAP Google Play data safety disclosure, and Google Privacy Policy.

The game keeps a local record of a one-way SHA-256 hash of each processed Google Play purchase token to prevent duplicate grants, along with its locally stored Detective Key balance. The full raw purchase token is not saved in this local reward ledger. A successful purchase is confirmed with the store only after its reward has been saved.

Consumable purchase limitation: Detective Keys and fulfilled-purchase records are stored on the device. Once a purchase is fulfilled and consumed, previously awarded Keys may not be recoverable after clearing app data, reinstalling without a usable backup, or moving to another device. Resetting story progress within the game preserves the separately stored Key balance and processed-purchase records.

4. Third-party platform services

Android, Google Play, the Unity game engine, Unity IAP and the included LevelPlay advertising service may handle technical information as part of app installation, operation, and optional ad delivery, subject to their notices and the services enabled in the release. The developer does not upload locally saved story progress or clue records to a developer-operated server.

For details about the platform providers, see the Google Privacy Policy and Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy. Those provider policies describe their broader practices; not every feature described in them is necessarily enabled in this game.

5. How locally stored information is used

Local game data is used to resume the story, display the casebook and clues, remember unlocked content and Detective Keys, save ad preferences and reward records, and preserve settings. The game does not require you to submit these records to the developer. Payment providers may handle the transaction information described above.

6. Advertising choices and privacy requests

You can decide whether to enable optional rewarded ads in the game through Menu > Settings > Ad settings. This choice is saved locally and, when relevant, communicated to LevelPlay through its supported consent API. By default, the game does not initialize its optional rewarded-ad service without your affirmative opt-in. You can change your choice later. Declining or turning off rewarded ads does not stop the main story or remove previously earned Detective Keys or unlocked Extra Stories.

Turning off rewarded ads stops the game from requesting new rewarded ads through this feature. It does not erase information already processed by a provider, uninstall an SDK, or necessarily affect an advertisement already in progress. The in-game preference does not replace any additional consent, opt-out, or privacy-rights procedures that may be required in your region.

If you have questions or wish to exercise applicable privacy rights, contact minimaldev.contact@gmail.com. Ad providers may offer separate controls and data-request processes in their own notices. Advertising preferences do not control Google Play's payment processing or Unity IAP data handling.

7. Retention, reset, and deletion

Local game progress and settings generally remain on the device until removed by an available in-game operation, clearing the app's data, or uninstalling the app, subject to Android backup and restore behavior.

Resetting story progress inside the game is not the same as deleting all app data. In-game story reset preserves separately stored Detective Keys, Extra Story and hint unlocks, purchase-reward ledger, and settings. To remove locally stored game data more completely, use your Android device's app-storage controls. Data processed by advertising, payment, and platform providers is subject to their own retention and deletion processes.

8. Security and international processing

The game does not send its local save files to a developer-operated cloud server. Advertising, payment, and platform providers may process information in countries other than your own according to their respective notices and safeguards. This policy does not guarantee any particular security or backup outcome.

9. Children and families

This game is not designed for or directed to children. Its declared target audience on Google Play does not include child age groups. The game does not ask players for their age, name, or contact information. If you are a parent or guardian with a privacy-related concern, please contact the developer using the email address below. This statement does not mean that third-party SDKs are incapable of processing device data; their practices are described above and in their linked policies.

10. Changes and contact

This policy may change if the game's features, third-party services, or applicable legal requirements change. The date above will be updated when a revised policy is published.

For privacy inquiries, contact minimaldev.contact@gmail.com.

This privacy policy applies only to the Android version of Animal School Mystery 3 — After-School Escape.
