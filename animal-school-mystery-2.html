Privacy Policy

Game: Animal School Mystery 2
Developer: YuSun Lim
Privacy contact: minimaldev.contact@gmail.com
Last updated: November 4, 2026

This policy describes how information is handled when you use Animal School Mystery 2 on Android. The game includes optional rewarded advertising and optional in-app purchases that provide Detective Keys. It is separate from the privacy policy for the first Animal School Mystery game.

1. Game data stored on your device

Animal School Mystery 2 does not require a player account. The app saves game progress, story and clue states, dialogue and investigation progress, detailed-hint unlocks, three Bonus Story unlocks and progress, Detective Key balance, selected language, sound preferences, and related settings locally on your device. The developer does not operate a game-account server or a remote database for these saves.

For purchase fulfillment and duplicate-reward prevention, the game stores local records of rewarded-ad grants and completed purchases. The purchase record contains a one-way SHA-256 hash derived from the Google Play purchase token; the raw token is not stored in the game's local purchase-reward history. Local saves may be lost when app data is cleared or the app is uninstalled, depending on device backup and restore behavior.

2. Optional rewarded advertising

The game integrates Unity LevelPlay for optional rewarded advertisements. Advertising delivery and mediation may involve Unity/ironSource and advertising-network partners enabled for this app. The set of ad networks that actually serves advertisements depends on the configuration and may change. The presence of an SDK or an adapter in the app package does not by itself establish that a particular network served an advertisement.

When advertising is enabled, advertising providers may process information such as advertising or device identifiers, approximate location or country, device and app information, interactions with advertisements, and diagnostic or performance information. Depending on the provider, these may be used for ad delivery, measurement, analytics, fraud prevention, and security. The developer does not transmit your locally saved story progress or clues to a developer-operated game server.

Relevant provider information: Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy, ironSource Ads Google Data Safety Questionnaire, and Unity Ads data safety disclosures.

Watching an advertisement is voluntary. When an eligible rewarded advertisement is completed and its reward is confirmed by the SDK, the game grants one Detective Key. Simply selecting advertising consent, tapping the ad button, or closing an advertisement without earning the reward does not grant a Key.

3. Optional in-app purchases

The game offers a voluntary, repeatable Support the Developer in-app purchase through Google Play. Each successfully fulfilled purchase grants five Detective Keys. This is a consumable product, not a subscription. Google Play displays the applicable price and currency for your account or region. Purchasing is not required to play the main story.

Google Play processes payments and associated transaction information. The game uses Unity In-App Purchasing (IAP) 5.4.4 to communicate with the store. According to Unity's disclosures for IAP 5.4 and later, the SDK may process purchase/transaction information, device and app identifiers, technical information, and approximate location such as country for purchase functionality and related services. See Unity IAP privacy overview, Unity IAP Google Play data safety disclosure, and Google Privacy Policy.

Detective Keys and purchase-fulfillment records are stored locally on this device. After a consumable purchase has been fulfilled and consumed by Google Play, Keys already granted are not guaranteed to be restored if app data is deleted, the app is reinstalled without a usable backup, or you switch devices. Resetting story progress within the game is not the same as deleting Android app data.

4. Data uses and third-party sharing

Local game data is used to continue the story, display earned rewards and progress, and preserve settings. Advertising-related information may be processed by Unity/ironSource and participating advertising providers to deliver and measure ads, analyze performance, and prevent abuse. Purchase-related information is processed by Google Play and Unity IAP to deliver and manage purchases. These services determine their own collection, sharing, and retention practices as explained in their policies. The developer does not operate a separate server collecting individual players' game saves.

5. Advertising choices and privacy requests

Rewarded ads are optional. In the game, open Menu > Settings > Ad Consent Settings to review or change your stored advertising choice. You can choose not to consent and still play the main story. Not consenting can prevent you from receiving rewards that require watching advertisements, but it does not remove Detective Keys or Bonus Stories you have already unlocked.

The game uses your saved advertising choice to determine whether it requests rewarded ads and to pass supported consent choices to the advertising SDK. If an SDK has already been initialized, changing a choice may not stop all provider-side processing immediately or erase data already processed. Advertising preferences do not control Google Play payment processing or Unity IAP data handling.

If you have privacy questions or wish to exercise rights that apply to you under local law, contact minimaldev.contact@gmail.com. Advertising, payment, and SDK providers may have additional privacy-choice and data-request processes described in their own notices.

6. Storage, retention, and deletion

Local story progress, Detective Keys, bonus and hint unlocks, and game settings generally remain on your device unless you clear app data, uninstall the game, or remove data through any available in-game reset features. Different reset actions may preserve certain Key or purchase records. The developer does not maintain a separate cloud backup of your progress or previously fulfilled consumable Keys. Third-party advertising, payment, and SDK providers manage data they process according to their own retention and deletion policies.

7. Security and international processing

The game does not upload its local story-save database to a developer-operated server. Third-party advertising and commerce services may process information in other countries under their own privacy policies. No specific security measure is guaranteed by this statement; the providers' published policies describe their respective security practices.

8. Children and intended audience

The game is not intended for children under 13. No player account or date-of-birth field is required by the game. Additional privacy requirements may apply to younger users according to local laws and the age settings established for the game on Google Play.

9. Policy updates and contact

This policy may be updated when the game, its advertising or payment services, or applicable privacy requirements change. Updates will be reflected by the date at the top of this page. Privacy inquiries: minimaldev.contact@gmail.com.

This policy covers Animal School Mystery 2 on Android only. The third-party policies linked above provide additional information about services used by the game.
